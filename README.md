Website for the Vibe&Ride project.\
\
Vibe&Ride is a workout music app that picks the best music for your current workout interval, focusing mainly on cycling workouts, but is not limited to them. Using song metadata and audio analysis from AcousticBrainz and GetSongBPM (getsongbpm.com), the app will pick the best personalized song for every moment of your workout.\
\
I started this initially as a student project. As a cyclist, I started developing the app for personal purposes, because I felt my workouts were missing something. I then decided to continue the development to develop a public app that is going to be available on Google Play soon (hopefully before the indoor riding season starts).
